Learn by doing

Projects & labs

Use these project starters to connect theory with hands-on skills.

BEGINNER

LED series circuit

Build a simple LED circuit and calculate an appropriate current-limiting resistor.

Skills: polarity, Ohm's Law, schematic reading.

BEGINNER

Resistor network

Build series and parallel resistor networks and compare calculated and measured values.

Skills: equivalent resistance, meter use.

INTERMEDIATE

Capacitor charge & discharge

Observe how a capacitor charges and discharges through a resistor in a low-voltage circuit.

Skills: time constants, graphing, measurement.

INTERMEDIATE

Transistor switch

Use a transistor as an electronic switch to control a suitable low-voltage load.

Skills: semiconductor basics, biasing, datasheets.

ADVANCED

Logic gate tester

Test basic logic gates and verify outputs against truth tables.

Skills: digital logic, truth tables, troubleshooting.

ADVANCED

Microcontroller sensor

Read a sensor and use the data to control an output on a microcontroller board.

Skills: inputs, outputs, coding, calibration.

Projects are planning prompts, not complete build instructions. Add your approved schematics, parts lists, voltage/current limits, risk assessments, and teacher-supervised procedures before student use.
At-a-glance help

Quick references

Core formulas and trusted starting points for electronics research.

Ohm's Law

V = I × R

Voltage = Current × Resistance

Electrical power

P = V × I

Power = Voltage × Current

Series resistance

Rₜ = R₁ + R₂ + …

Add resistances in a series path.

Parallel resistance

1/Rₜ = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂ + …

For two resistors: Rₜ = (R₁R₂)/(R₁+R₂).
